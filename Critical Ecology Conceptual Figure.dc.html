CONCEPTUAL FIGURE

Power as a quantitative driver of ecosystem change

Start by reading this interactive conceptual figure from left to right. Power, a feature of all societies, determines what groups have the agency to affect natural systems in consequential ways. The results ripple through the structure and function of ecosystems. The arcs beneath represent feedback effects. Hover any element to read what it means, see examples of what we might measure to understand this part of the Earth system, and what traditional global change sciences leaves out.
