MISSION STATEMENT

The Critical Ecology Lab is a startup environmental research institution, people’s think tank, and creative community hub.

We investigate and explain the ways that the natural world, from soils to the atmosphere, has been shaped by the ideas of racial and cultural supremacy, natural resource exploitation, and social exclusion.
